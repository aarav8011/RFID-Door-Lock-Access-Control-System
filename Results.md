Results and Expected Output

Expected Results

1. The RFID reader scans the RFID card.
2. The Arduino checks the card UID.
3. If the card is authorized, access is granted.
4. The servo motor rotates to unlock the door.
5. After three seconds, the door returns to the locked position.
6. If the card is unauthorized, access is denied.

Conclusion

The RFID Door Lock Access Control System provides a simple method of controlling door access using RFID technology and Arduino.
